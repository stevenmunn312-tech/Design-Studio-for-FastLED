## Product name

Design Studio for FastLED

## Description

Limit 10,000 characters.

```text
Design Studio for FastLED is a visual workshop for LED projects. Build effects for LED strips, rings, matrices and tiled panels by connecting nodes, watch them run live on screen, then turn the same design into real FastLED firmware and upload it to your microcontroller over USB.

No blank sketch, no guesswork. Patterns, palettes, signals, effects, audio analysis and hardware output are all nodes. Wire them together, adjust a speed or a colour, and the preview responds at once. When it looks right on screen, it is the firmware your board runs: the preview and the C++ generator are built together so the LEDs follow what you designed.

CREATE
- More than 200 modules: patterns, simulations, colour and palettes, fields, effects, particles, logic, show control, audio, hardware inputs and outputs.
- Start Gallery templates for fire, scrolling text, live audio, field warping, generative shows, music playback and physical controls, each with notes on what to try next.
- 100 bundled audio-reactive patterns, plus your own Pattern Library: turn any patch into a reusable group and organise it on shelves.

PERFORM
- Stage View turns the workspace into a full-screen operator view with spectrum, transport and frame rate.
- Music-synced shows, beat-driven particles, transitions and a Control Deck for live adjustment.
- Audio-reactive effects from your microphone, or from music played by the board itself from SD card, internet radio or Bluetooth.

BUILD THE HARDWARE
- A hardware workbench: pick your exact board, add the parts you own, and get filtered pin pickers, bus checks and wiring diagnostics before you upload.
- Power and fuse sizing for your LED load, with measured flash and RAM use for each build.
- A printable Build Diagram with the board's real pin map, power distribution, parts list and connection list.
- Buttons, knobs, encoders, sensors, OLED, TFT and touch screens, segment displays, and a Web Remote that serves a phone control page from an ESP32.

DEPLOY
- Generate a standalone Arduino sketch (.ino), or compile and flash your board directly over USB.
- Stream live frames from the Studio to a connected controller.
- Record the preview as PNG, GIF or WebM to share your work.

BOARDS
Tested end to end on ESP32, ESP32-S3 and ESP8266 boards with WS2812B LEDs. Builds are also offered for RP2040 and Raspberry Pi Pico, Teensy, AVR, SAMD, STM32, nRF52 and Arduino UNO R4 boards; these are experimental until they are verified on hardware.

GOOD TO KNOW
- The first time you build for a board, the app downloads the compiler, board support package and FastLED library it needs (Arduino CLI, ESP32 and RP2040 cores, FastLED). After that, it works offline.
- Uploading needs a USB connection to a microcontroller board. Designing and previewing need nothing but the app.
- Your projects are saved in your Documents folder. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics, and sends nothing to the developer.

Design Studio for FastLED is free and open source. FastLED is a separate open-source library by its own authors; this app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FastLED project.

LED installations can draw substantial current. Use a correctly rated power supply and fuses, and never power a large LED load through a microcontroller or USB port.
```
