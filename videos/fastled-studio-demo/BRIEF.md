## Intent

A fast, polished demo-tour of the real app. Open on a vivid finished LED look,
pull back to the node graph, show a fresh patch being built and tuned with an
instant preview, then close on generated FastLED code and hardware-ready output.
The creative core is the app's own signal flow: idea to nodes to light.

## Customizations

- Use only newly recorded app footage and newly created narration, music, sound
  effects, and motion graphics for this video.
- Keep the app's real UI on screen throughout the demonstration; use motion
  framing and callouts to make it feel like a launch piece rather than a slow
  tutorial.
- Follow the signal visually through the patch so the graph's connected nature
  becomes the editing motif.

## Notes

- Do not reuse existing tutorial videos, title cards, screenshots, narration,
  music, or other pre-existing media in this repository.
- Capture a clean, fresh browser session without deleting or altering the
  user's saved projects, patterns, or Chrome profile.
- Avoid a full-screen static UI shot for long stretches. At 60 seconds, the
  dense desktop interface needs purposeful crops and short holds so labels stay
  readable at 1920x1080.
- Do not trigger a real device upload, SD write, or hardware flash.
