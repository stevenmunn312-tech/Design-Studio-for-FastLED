## Overview

The frame is a cinematic extension of the Design Studio workspace: a precise,
dark lighting console with visible but restrained signal color. It should feel
technical, creative, and approachable.

## Composition rules

- Anchor the brand and typography to the left and the animated node graph to
  the right.
- Keep two clear focal points: the hero title and the illuminated output
  matrix.
- Use one dominant motion idea: a left-to-right signal pulse.
- Keep all important copy inside the 150px title-safe margin.
- Use large video-scale typography and details at least two pixels thick.
- Use the exact colors and type families in the frontmatter.

## Do

- Use subtle grid lines, structural rules, node panels, round ports, and LED
  pixels that clearly reference the application.
- Let cyan Frame signals pass through purple Show elements into magenta Output.
- Use lime only when the tutorial is explicitly about audio.
- Hold the final hero frame completely stable for editorial flexibility.

## Do not

- Do not use generic cyberpunk HUDs, glitch effects, scan lines, dense
  circuitry, metallic textures, heavy fog, or full-screen linear gradients.
- Do not animate every element independently.
- Do not redraw or recolor the official pixel brand lockup.
- Do not place text over the node illustration.
